# INC-231: price list fetch fails behind the proxy

- Seen with httpcore 1.0.0: CONNECT tunnels through the corporate proxy
  were closed early; every fetch through the proxy failed.
- Workaround: pin `httpcore<1.0` (done in fetcher 0.5.2).
- Upstream fix: httpcore 1.0.2 ("Fix proxy tunnel teardown").
- Follow-up: lift the pin once httpcore 1.0.2 or later is on the mirror.
